# **About Me**

Hi I am **{{ site.author.name }}** :wave:,<br>
I'm a firmware and hardware engineer based in the Bay Area. I graduated from the University of Waterloo in 2025 with a BASc in Mechanical Engineering (with Distinction) and minors in Mechatronics, Computing and AI, but my curiosity has always pulled me across engineering disciplines. Most recently I was a firmware engineer at Level Home, where I built the embedded Linux BSP and A/B OTA update system for a networking hub and improved battery estimation across 225K deployed units. Before that, I worked on Tesla's Optimus humanoid robot and designed production PCBs and firmware at Edge AI Solutions. I thrive where electrical, firmware and mechanical engineering meet, which is probably why I love robotics so much! I was also the Electrical Lead and Team Captain of the University of Waterloo Robotics Team from 2021 to 2024.
